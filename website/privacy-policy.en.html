Privacy policy

Updated 20 November 2026 · YourSafe

What stays on your PC

You can use YourSafe without an online account. The Windows app saves the information you add, including passwords, website addresses, notes, verification code settings and recovery codes, in an encrypted vault on your PC. YourSafe does not sync your vault to the cloud.

What the browser extension can access

Browser integration is optional. When it is enabled and the vault is unlocked, opening the extension uses the current page address to find matching accounts. The extension can show account names, usernames and whether a password or verification code is available.

The extension can check the selected page and find its login fields. Filling a login, viewing a verification code and copying a verification code each require your approval in the Windows app. If you change pages, the extension cancels the request. It does not collect the full page or keep your browsing history.

Who can see your data

YourSafe uses vault and page information on your PC to provide these features. The Windows app and extension do not send this information or usage reports to the developer. YourSafe does not sell the information, use it for advertising or keep a copy of your vault for recovery.

When you approve autofill, the selected website can read the username and password placed in its form. You decide when to submit the form.

The extension follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

What you can manage or delete

The extension uses information temporarily and does not keep a copy of your passwords or vault. A verification code can stay visible until it expires or you close its view. Windows clipboard history or sync can retain information you copy.

You can edit or delete saved accounts, turn off browser integration, or remove the extension. Removing the Windows app leaves the vault on your PC. You decide when to delete the vault and any backups you exported.

Website visits and support

If you check for updates in the Windows app or enable daily checks, YourSafe asks GitHub for public release information. The app compares versions on your PC without sending your installed version or vault data. GitHub may process connection information under its privacy policy.

This website uses Google Analytics to count visits and clicks on selected links. Google may use cookies and receive information about your browser and device. Learn how Google uses this information.

The Windows app, extension and demo login page do not use Google Analytics. The demo keeps what you type in your browser without saving or sending it. GitHub hosts this website and may process connection information under its privacy policy.

If you email us or post on GitHub, we use what you share to respond. Anyone can read public GitHub issues. Keep passwords, verification codes, recovery keys and vault files out of support messages. You can ask us to delete support information we still hold. Third-party services apply their own privacy policies.
