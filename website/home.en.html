BROWSER EXTENSION

Passwords on your PC.
Autofill in your browser.

Open the YourSafe extension on a login page, choose an account, and click Fill to fill the username and password. Approve the request in YourSafe on Windows. The extension fills the fields; you click Sign in.

If an account has TOTP set up in YourSafe, you can view or copy its verification code in the extension. Each view or copy needs a separate approval in the Windows app.

Select Open YourSafe in the extension to start the Windows app or bring it forward. Unlock the vault, then enable browser integration in Settings.

LOCAL STORAGE

Where are your
passwords stored?

Your encrypted vault is stored on your Windows PC. YourSafe does not sync it to the cloud or connect to the internet. Export a backup and keep it on a USB drive or another device in case your PC fails.

Sign in and unlock your vault

Sign in with your Master Password and a six-digit code from your authenticator app. If the vault locks while your login session is still valid, only the Master Password is needed to unlock it again.

Once installed, find YourSafe in the Start Menu to open the app.

Using the portable ZIP

Right-click the ZIP file, choose Extract All, then open YourSafe.exe in the extracted folder.

Keep all the files together. Do not run the app from inside the ZIP.

When you create a vault, save the Recovery Key somewhere safe away from this PC. Then try saving an account and export a backup to a USB drive or another device.

Common questions

What if I can’t download or open the YourSafe installer?

If the download fails: Try the official download button again. If it still fails, download the ZIP from Assets on GitHub, extract all files, then open YourSafe.exe.

If Windows blocks the installer after download: If the warning names Smart App Control, check Windows Security → App & browser control → Smart App Control settings. Keep it on where possible. If you’ve verified the file against the official release and still choose to disable the feature, select Off and try opening the installer again. This reduces protection across your PC. Windows may also block the executable in the ZIP.

Do I need internet access or an online account?

No. You can save, find, and view passwords offline without an online account. YourSafe does not sync your vault or send usage reports. A connection is needed to download the app or check for updates. You can check in Settings or turn on daily checks.

What if I forget my Master Password or lose my authenticator?

If you still have the vault files and the Recovery Key, you can reset the Master Password and set up a new authenticator. Without the key, you need a usable encrypted backup and its passphrase to recover your data. YourSafe has no online copy to recover for you.

How do I recover my data on another PC or after reinstalling Windows?

Restore an encrypted backup using its passphrase. Then set a new Master Password and authenticator for the vault on the new installation.

Copying the app folder does not copy your vault. The Recovery Key cannot replace a backup if the PC or drive is lost. A backup only contains data saved at the time it was exported, so back up regularly and keep it on another device.

Do I need the browser extension?

No. You can save, find, view, and copy account details in the Windows app. The optional extension adds autofill in Chrome, Edge, and Brave.

Open YourSafe on Windows, unlock the vault, and enable browser integration in Settings. Enable it again each time you start the app. On a login page, open the extension, choose an account, and click Fill. Approve the request in the Windows app, then submit the login form yourself. Read about the browser extension.

Why can’t the extension find or fill an account?

Use Open YourSafe to open the Windows app, then unlock the vault and check browser integration in Settings. The saved website must match the site you are visiting. The item editor indicates whether the URL can be used for autofill. Accounts without a website address do not appear in the extension.

Reopen or refresh the extension, choose an account, and approve the request in the Windows app. The extension fills the fields; you submit the form.

Can I import passwords from a browser or another app?

Yes. Export a CSV from your old app, then use CSV import in YourSafe. You can review the data before importing. Accounts identified as duplicates are not overwritten.

CSV files can contain readable passwords. After checking the imported data, remove temporary CSV files from places where you do not want to keep them.

Why does the vault lock while I’m using it?

The vault locks after a fixed time from unlocking: one minute by default. Using the app does not extend that time. The vault shows the time remaining. You can change the duration in Settings.

While your login session is valid, only the Master Password is needed to unlock the vault again. After the session ends, at most five hours later, or you restart the app, sign in again with the Master Password and an authenticator code.

What if my PC has malware?

Encryption protects the data saved in your vault. When the vault is unlocked, malware can still read data or capture the screen. Keep your PC secure, use a strong Master Password, and keep backups on another device.

Does uninstalling the app delete my passwords?

Uninstalling leaves the vault on your PC. If you also want to delete the vault, make sure you have a working backup before removing the data yourself.
